### Consistent Quartz



In real life, amethyst is just a violet variety of quartz, but in vanilla Minecraft, amethyst and quartz have entirely different mechanics. The purpose of this mod is to bridge the gap between them.

Amethyst gets a block set to match the quartz block set, and Nether quartz ore is removed and replaced with quartz geodes.

Quartz geodes generate exclusively in the Nether.

Budding quartz functions identically to budding amethyst, which means it's unfortunately unobtainable in Survival.

Quartz buds have 4 growth stages identical to that of amethyst buds, and they glow with the same level of brightness. Quartz clusters drop Nether quartz, which can be used to craft quartz crystal blocks, just like how amethyst blocks are crafted from 4 amethyst shards.

The vanilla block of quartz can now be crafted from 4 quartz crystal blocks.

Crafting 4 amethyst crystal blocks will give you polished amethyst. For consistency, block of quartz is also renamed to "polished quartz" (its slabs and stairs are renamed as well).

Both of the crystal blocks also have slab and stair variants, which make amethyst chime sounds when you walk on them!

The rest of the blocks added by the mod are simply purple amethyst variants of the quartz block set.
